## Summary of Peer Review Remediation

We thank the reviewer for the rigorous technical and methodological audit of `v1.0.1`. Every technical gap, architectural omission, and editorial inconsistency identified has been addressed in the `v1.1.0` engineering revision, leaving only physical Debian 13 hardware benchmark capture as the final pre-release step before minting the permanent `v1.1.0` DOI.
